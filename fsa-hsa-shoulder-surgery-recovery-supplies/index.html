Which Shoulder Surgery Recovery Supplies Are FSA or HSA Eligible?

By James Park · November 2026

An FSA is money you set aside through your employer before tax; an HSA is your own account tied to a high-deductible health plan, and it never expires. Both can pay for shoulder surgery costs, but what counts as "eligible" depends on your specific plan's list and, underneath that, on an IRS test about medical necessity. What should your FSA or HSA cover first?

The surgery bill itself. Your copay, coinsurance, deductible, and physical therapy sessions are all medical care under IRS Publication 502, and they're costs you'll owe regardless, so they come ahead of optional gear. FSAFEDS marks "Surgery," "Medical (Co-insurance, Co-Payment, Deductible)," and "Physical therapy" as eligible with a detailed receipt. Ask your insurer for your estimated share before you plan a supply budget around what's left.

Which recovery supplies are FSA or HSA eligible?

It depends on which list your plan actually uses, since FSAFEDS and WEX don't always agree. Over-the-counter pain relievers, bandages, first aid kits, and TENS units are eligible with a receipt on FSAFEDS. Arm slings, cold or hot packs sold as medical supplies, and heating pads appear as eligible on WEX but have no FSAFEDS entry at all. A sling or immobilizer is often needed for weeks after shoulder arthroscopy or rotator cuff repair, according to AAOS OrthoInfo, so that item is worth checking first.

Note

The underlying IRS test (Publication 502) is whether something is "primarily to alleviate or prevent a physical or mental disability or illness," not just generally helpful. That's why a cold pack sold to chill drinks won't qualify even though an identical-looking one sold as a medical supply might.

Why do cold therapy machines and recliners get conflicting answers?

Because each list treats everyday-use items differently, and neither public source explains why. FSAFEDS asks for a letter of medical necessity for both a cold therapy unit and a recliner (capped at $1,000), while WEX lists cold units as simply "Eligible" and recliner chairs as "Not Eligible." AAOS OrthoInfo notes that lying flat doesn't affect healing after shoulder arthroscopy, but some patients are more comfortable reclined or propped up in the first days, which is likely why recliners show up on these lists at all.

What paperwork do you actually need to keep?

An itemized receipt, and sometimes a doctor's letter. FSAFEDS states plainly that credit card receipts and balance-forward statements don't meet its documentation standard. For items flagged "Rx/MN" (prescription or medical necessity) on WEX, or anything FSAFEDS requires a letter for, get that note from your surgeon before you buy, not after.

Tip

If you're unsure whether a shoulder-support pillow or similar item qualifies, call your plan administrator before ordering rather than after — a rejected claim after the return window closes can't be fixed.

What happens to unused FSA money at year-end?

It's generally forfeited unless your plan offers a grace period or a carryover, never both. A grace period gives you up to 2½ months past the plan year to spend the old balance; a carryover moves up to $680 of unused 2026 money into 2027. HSA funds are different — they roll over indefinitely and can reimburse a qualified expense any time after the account was opened, with no deadline, per IRS Notice 2004-50.

Does surgery timing change which year's funds you use?

Yes, generally the expense is dated to when care is provided, not when you're billed. FSAFEDS says reimbursement is "based on when an expense is incurred, not when it is paid." A late-2026 surgery with 2027 physical therapy can split across two plan years, so check your plan's rules before assuming either balance applies. Frequently asked questions

Is a shoulder sling FSA or HSA eligible?

It may be, depending on your plan's list. WEX lists "Arm Sling" as eligible, while FSAFEDS has no specific sling entry, though its "Orthopedic & surgical supports" category might apply — your administrator decides. Keep the itemized receipt regardless, and check separately if your surgeon prescribes a shoulder immobilizer instead of a basic sling.

Can I buy a recliner with FSA money for shoulder surgery recovery?

Possibly, but the two major reference lists disagree. FSAFEDS allows up to $1,000 with a letter of medical necessity, while WEX lists recliner chairs as not eligible except in rare prescribed cases. Ask your plan administrator directly before purchasing, since the $1,000 cap is a plan rule, not an IRS limit.

Do I need a doctor's note for recovery supplies?

Not for over-the-counter items like pain relievers, which are covered whether or not prescribed. Equipment like recliners, cold therapy machines, and some support cushions often needs a letter of medical necessity or prescription, so ask before buying rather than after.

What happens if I don't use all my FSA money by December?

It may be forfeited unless your plan has a grace period (up to 2½ months) or a carryover (up to $680 for 2026), and plans offer one or the other, never both. HSA balances don't face this issue since they carry over every year with no expiration.

Is there a deadline to reimburse myself from an HSA?

No. IRS Notice 2004-50 confirms there's no time limit, as long as the expense was incurred after the HSA existed and you keep records proving it qualifies. Unlike FSA claims, HSA documentation stays with your own tax records rather than being submitted for approval.
